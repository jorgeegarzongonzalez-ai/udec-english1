Welcome to your English coach

This is your personal, English-only training space. First, set up your profile and your access code. The whole experience is in English — that is part of the training.

Your access code is free and powers your coach. It is stored only in your own browser on this device.
How do I get my access code?
  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click the blue “Create” button (or “Crear clave”).
  3. A long code will appear. Press Copy to copy the whole code.
  4. Come back here and paste it in the box above.

You do this only once. Keep your code — you can reuse it every session.
